# Momo: four flavours, one table

Production brief · 5 October 2026

This is a film brief, not a generated movie. The old slideshow has been retired.

## Tool and reference

Use [Google Flow](https://flow.google.com/) or [Runway](https://app.runwayml.com/). Runway’s current Gen-4.5 guide supports 2–10-second clips. Check the options and cost in your own account. No paid generation has been started.

Use `momo-flavours.png` as a character reference. Build a scene-specific first frame rather than animating the horizontal lineup. The desired result has independently moving characters, physical props and a changing camera perspective.

## Master prompt

Create a 5-second cinematic animated short with four clearly different original food characters: a white pleated steamed momo, a golden blistered deep-fried momo, an orange-red charred tandoori momo with herbs, and a glossy red Schezwan momo with sesame. Use the supplied crayon character sheet for colours and identity, translated into tactile stop-motion clay with hand-painted pencil edges. This is actual character animation with expressive faces, independently moving limbs and a moving camera, not a slideshow or a camera pan over a still image.

0.0–1.2 seconds: begin in a miniature office at blue hour. The steamed momo closes a laptop. Its warm desk lamp becomes a match-cut to a café lamp.
1.2–3.4 seconds: one continuous low tracking shot follows the fried, tandoori and Schezwan momos arriving from different streets, opening a café door and pulling out chairs. Every character remains its own distinct food type.
3.4–5.0 seconds: camera arcs around the small table as the steamed momo joins. A card lands on the table, one friend slides the chutney over, a glance becomes a laugh. End with a deliberate empty chair in the foreground, inviting the viewer in.

Warm practical lighting, evening rain reflections, shallow depth of field, handcrafted miniature sets, believable weight and contact, gently imperfect stop-motion timing. Friendly adults’ nostalgia, no baby voices. Sound: laptop click, café bell, chair scrape, card tap, soft laughter. Compose for 9:16 with subjects inside a central crop-safe area; also render 16:9. Keep the last 0.7 seconds visually calm for the editor to add “kal milte hain?” and the Momo wordmark. Render without generated text.

## Shot-by-shot workflow

| Shot | Final edit | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A small decision | 0–1.2 sec | A working-day action ends; a face responds to the thought of a plan. |
| The way there | 1.2–3.4 sec | Low tracking shot into a warm café; distinct characters move with believable weight. |
| The shared table | 3.4–5 sec | A plate passes, a card lands, a small laugh, an empty chair for the viewer. |

Generate each shot separately with extra duration, then select the strongest five seconds. For image-to-video, focus the prompt on motion and camera. If too much happens to read clearly, simplify the middle shot instead of speeding everything up.

## Delivery and review

- Separate 9:16 Instagram and 16:9 website compositions. Keep faces and text crop-safe.
- Original or licensed sound only: café bell, footsteps, chair scrape, card shuffle. No recognisable film songs without rights.
- Add “kal milte hain?” and the wordmark in the editor; do not rely on generated text.
- Export a full-quality master plus H.264 web copy, with a muted website version.
- Reject still-image pans, food morphing, cloned faces, extra limbs, flickering props or fake celebrity cameos.
- Do not label anyone lazy, shy or awkward. The film invites people; it does not diagnose them.
- Only add an actual date, venue and cost once confirmed. Label concept posts as a preview.
