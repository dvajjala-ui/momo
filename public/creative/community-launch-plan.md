# Momo — the first table, then the following

Ahmedabad working launch plan · 5 October 2026

## Brand

A friend making a small weekend plan. Good food, a game, and a story to take home. A childhood notebook made by an adult for other adults. A person can talk, listen or leave a silence alone. No personality labels or promises to fix people.

Momo edition: steamed, fried, tandoori and Schezwan. Whole-menu edition: pizza, vada pav, noodles, pasta, chai/coffee and dal baati. Same community and invite list, two visual worlds.

Original little lines: “kal milte hain?”, “the group chat finally made it out”, “we’ve had enough meetings. let’s meet”, “one last round, allegedly”. Joke about situations, not identities, atrocities or someone spending time alone. Avoid dictator references, gender bait and fake urgency.

## What comparable products do well

Timeleft explains the small-group format and public venue arrangements. Its safety material discusses verification limits, reporting and women-only options. Borrow the clarity, not its branding or unearned safety claims.

Bumble BFF makes reporting and blocking visible and publishes conduct rules for both messages and gatherings. Moderation is a host responsibility, not a decorative badge.

These sources do not prove what will attract women in Ahmedabad. The following are local hypotheses to validate, not demographic facts.

## Make women want to return

Ask 5–8 adult women from your existing circles to review the idea privately: what would they need to know, what venue/time works, and how would they want a concern handled? Do not treat women as inventory to attract men.

Recruit a woman co-host who wants a genuine role in shaping the event, with fair payment or agreed credit. Show the actual hosts, public venue, start/end time, group size, full cost and contact route before commitment. Trial a bring-a-friend option and daylight or early-evening café plans. If participants want it, offer a women-only gathering with an appropriate host. Never imply a gender ratio that has not been established.

Keep phone numbers private. Do not automatically add people to WhatsApp groups. Keep one-to-one chat deferred until there is a clear consent model. Let anyone decline a photo or leave early without explanation. Never turn a refusal into content.

## Instagram

Start one account, not two fragmented audiences. Handle ideas: `kal.milte.hain`, `momo.weekends`, `momo.tableclub`. Availability is not verified. Display name: “Momo | Ahmedabad weekend tables”.

Bio draft:

> Good food. A game or two. Kal milte hain? 🖍️
> Small, hosted get-togethers in Ahmedabad · 18+
> The full plan first. Your choice after.

Pinned posts: “What actually happens?” / “Who’s hosting?” / “Different fillings. Same table.” Highlights: The plan / Hosts / Cost / Table rules / Questions / Memories. Use only real, consented meetup photos in Memories.

Do not advertise a private Site as a public signup page. Open access deliberately after the host workflow and operator/privacy details are ready.

## Two-week pilot

| Days | Work | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Review with prospective participants and co-host. | Honest founder note explaining the idea. |
| 3–4 | Confirm public café, access, timing, cost and group size. | Real venue walkthrough and both invitation postcards. |
| 5–6 | Explain signup flow; approach relevant communities with permission. | “Where does my request go?” plus a café collaboration post. |
| 7–9 | Publish first character film; review opted-in requests. | Genuine availability only, no fake countdown. |
| 10 | Confirm participants and practical arrangements. | Private message with venue, end time and host contact. |
| 11 | Run the pilot and ask photo preferences separately. | No obligation to appear in content. |
| 12 | Ask privately what worked and what should change. | Internal feedback. |
| 13–14 | Share approved memories and the next real plan. | A short story from the table. |

These are proposed relative days, not scheduled automations. No account was created and no outreach was sent.

## Content worth sharing

- Reel: an UNO card hits the table, everyone reacts, “one last round, allegedly”. Only with permission.
- Carousel: a workday, an empty Saturday, a handwritten invitation, a real café table.
- Story poll: momos / vada pav / pasta / chai. Let the result influence a real plan.
- Host video: 20 seconds explaining venue, cost, timing, and bringing a friend.
- Afterward: food and small details before faces. Ask separately before posting identifiable people.

Start distribution with café collaborations, adult book/board-game/hobby groups, and people who already trust the hosts. Seek community owners’ permission to post. No mass DMs, attendee-list scraping, or unsolicited WhatsApp additions.

## Measure the experience

Track requests, invitation acceptance, attendance and voluntary repeat attendance. Ask whether the description was accurate, whether saying no felt comfortable, and what should change. Measure response to reports, not just followers. Optional demographic feedback should have a clear purpose; never infer gender from names/photos or publish identifiable small-group statistics.

## Public-launch checks

Finalize organizer identity, privacy contact, retention and complaint handling, venue/food arrangements, cancellations and public image rights. The supplied movie stills and memes are now used in the private inspiration scrapbook. They are not licensed brand campaign assets or evidence of past meetups.
