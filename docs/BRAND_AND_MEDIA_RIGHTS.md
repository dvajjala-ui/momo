# Brand and media rights status

Last checked: 14 October 2026

This records availability signals and the operating rules for Momo to Pizza. It is not trademark clearance or legal advice. Availability can change until a name, domain or handle is actually registered.

## Name search

- An open-web search found no obvious exact use of **Momo to Pizza** for an Ahmedabad meetup service.
- **MOMO** is a crowded term in Indian food and hospitality marks. An indexed IP India decision for `Yo! MOMO` describes an earlier similar `YO MOMOS` registration in restaurant/food services. The exact phrase result does not remove similarity risk.
- IP India's current public trademark search requires OTP and CAPTCHA. A complete exact/similar-mark search was not submitted during this check.
- Before filing or investing in promotion, search word and device marks in the classes covering the actual service, likely including entertainment/community events and any food/venue service, and have an Indian trademark professional assess similarity and scope.

Official search entry: https://ipindia.gov.in/trade-marks-before-you-apply-search-existing-trademarks

## Participant photographs

Do not publish an identifiable participant solely because a host checked a general gallery-rights box. Before publishing each image:

1. identify every recognizable adult in the image;
2. record their permission for that exact image and the intended site/social channels;
3. record the permission date, retention/reuse period and withdrawal contact;
4. keep anyone who declined out of frame or blur them before publication;
5. remove controlled copies when permission is withdrawn and record what happened to copies already shared externally.

Use `PARTICIPANT_PHOTO_PERMISSION.md` as the private operator template. Completed records and participant contact details must remain outside Git.
