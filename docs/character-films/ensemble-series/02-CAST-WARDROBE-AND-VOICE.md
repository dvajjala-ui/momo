## Two-person staging and voice rules

Use one speaker at a time unless a named overlap is intentional. A two-line exchange in an eight-second shot generally leaves 0.4 seconds before speech, around 1.5–2.8 seconds per short line, a listening beat and a settling beat. These are estimates, not recorded durations. Give longer lines their own shot. Do not accelerate voices to force a target. Exact words and line IDs live in each episode’s voice ledger. Hindi/Hinglish scripts are spoken as written once, not followed by an English explanation.

Record questions, replies, laughs and breaths as separately replaceable pieces. Keep the same chosen permitted voice per person across origin films, ensemble episodes and ads. Casting details and future credential handling are in [shared voice preparation](../shared/01-VOICE-PRODUCTION.md). No key or real provider ID is in these documents.
