## L01 — fictional University-area courtyard café

The official Zen Café page lists an Ahmedabad location at Amdavad ni Gufa on University Road and other city branches. This supports the idea of a café culture around that area; **E01's venue is an original fictional courtyard café, not a depiction of Zen Café or a claim that it hosts Momo.** [Zen Café's own location page](https://www.zencafe.co/in/slowdown.html).

Design our set with a low plaster wall, shaded plants, plain wood/metal tables, mismatched but stable adult chairs, a small counter in soft focus, exposed concrete and warm practical bulbs. A doorway opens toward an ordinary city street with scooters heard at a distance, not an empty luxury resort. Give one table a visible paper coaster and a slightly short leg for E01. Late-afternoon shade has warm edge light and cooler faces. Original menu graphics are added later; no real café logo, invented quoted price or assumed momo menu at a real business. No loud soundtrack from a fictional speaker competes with dialogue.

Use its distinctive colorful structural rhythm and walking context, not a generic road bridge. [SRFDCL: Atal Bridge](https://www.sabarmatiriverfront.com/atal-bridge.html).

For E02, stage the group on a broad permitted pedestrian area at late afternoon, with a stable rail in the background and an unobstructed walking lane. Make a reference layout before close shots. Breeze moves hair tips and shirt hems toward one fixed screen direction. Water/sky, distant city movement and light footfalls establish the setting; no mountain peaks or ocean waves. Never put a parked scooter or driving car on the pedestrian bridge. The exact entry point, current access conditions, operating time and cost are not locked by this script; check them when scheduling a real visit. Photo action does not require climbing a rail, blocking walkers or flying a drone.

## Atmosphere that belongs to a city

Use a few truthful details at once: a ceiling fan slowing a paper corner, sunlit dust outside a window, a steel water bottle touching a tabletop, an auto passing beyond the doorway, leaves on an urban path, ordinary multilingual sign shapes rendered legibly later. No continuous horn wall, exoticizing music, decorative poverty or identical landmark collage. A mild winter breeze is not alpine fog; monsoon cloud light is not a sudden night sky.
