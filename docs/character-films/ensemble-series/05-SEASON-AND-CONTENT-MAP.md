# Season and campaign map

## Three kinds of story, with different jobs

| Format | Length | Job | Brand placement |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Individual character film | 2:12–2:40 | Meet a person, their ordinary day and a small choice to go out | A relevant plan/letter plus restrained closing mark |
| Ensemble episode | 2:08 or 2:24 | Let an actual fictional friendship develop through a place/activity | Usually a small closing mark; no feature recital |
| Conversation short | 0:24–0:32 | One recognizable exchange, a laugh or a permission to be ordinary | Optional quiet series mark |
| Brand ad | 0:15 / 0:30 / 0:45 / 1:00 | Give one clear reason to explore the brand | One readable CTA, never fake event/social proof |

## Two additional future-episode concepts

These extend the eight fully scripted episodes to a ten-idea development bank. They are outlines, not complete shot plans or additional promised finished scripts.

- **E09 — Bus Stop Wali Baat:** Two friends miss a connection by a minute and use the wait to compare the very different playlists that get them through a commute. They ask staff before moving an apparently forgotten book. The ending is a shared recommendation, not taking somebody else’s property or turning the café into an unannounced dating event.

## Other brand material that can grow from this writing

- **Invitation motion card, 8–12 seconds:** Original folded paper opens, a name/date field appears only after real details exist, and one small steam/sparkle accent settles. Hero, princess and notebook treatments are optional preferences available to any person, not gender-assigned boxes. Do not borrow recognizable superhero insignia.
- **City chapter bumper, 5–8 seconds:** Original painted landmark silhouette, one small character walk-through and a readable chapter label. Use researched geography; no invented café partnership logos.
- **Character introduction card, 10–15 seconds:** One signature action from a reviewed character shot, their first name and a simple current desire such as “Aaj kuch naya.” No diagnoses, personality labels or body joke.
- **End-of-outing memory card, 8–12 seconds:** An accepted fictional photo/cast frame inside an original scrapbook border, one short personal line and a small brand mark. For a real participant version, use only their chosen image and reviewed words.
- **Season recap, 45–60 seconds:** Select accepted shots with dates/weather consistent; preserve the listening reaction that makes each moment mean something. This is a later edit from produced material, not a new claim that footage exists now.

These are additional production concepts. The eight episode packs, six conversation files and four ad files contain the full detailed scripts currently written.

It tests identity consistency and human interaction. Keep that first batch review separate from future spending or voice generation; the present deliverable is writing only.
