# Meera — A Seat in the Audience

**Production brief v1 · 5 November 2026 · Target 160 seconds / 2:40 · 32 shots · Six editable batches.** No character image, video, final voice or music has been generated for this long-form plan. The supplied micro-designs and supporting cast are proposals for review, not already approved assets.

Meera, a 24-year-old Indian woman, practises Bharatanatyam and enjoys badminton. Finding a few people for a game leads to repeated small meetings, then a more personal invitation: her dance performance. The payoff is not a tournament win or an instantly guaranteed friend group. It is the surprise of seeing familiar people quietly take seats in the audience, and a laugh over tea afterward.

The story restores both halves of the owner's original request: **badminton/hanging out and the group attending her Bharatanatyam performance**. The older badminton-tournament-only ending is not used. Several Sundays and a later performance date are separated in the edit, so the connection is earned rather than compressed into one magical afternoon.

## What is fixed and what is proposed

The adult lead, Indian setting, two activities, gradual connection and friends at her performance come from the owner's story. Athletic/petite wording and teal badminton clothes came from the earlier series draft. The exact face, 158 cm working scale, practice clothes, stage palette, supporting friends Nisha/Aarav, individual dialogue and 160-second edit are this plan's **working creative choices**. They can be revised before expensive production.

The new Meera package follows the pulled series' proposed original cinematic 3D drawing/rendering language. This does not convert Tara's approved 2D character or establish a finished matching ensemble. Keep Meera consistent within her film; any cross-film adaptation needs a separate visual review.

## Starting a batch

Read the character bible once, then use the appropriate exact wardrobe block with one shot prompt. The shot ranges describe the **edit**, not a promise that a generator accepts five-second raw takes. Review the first performance/prop test before committing a whole batch. Keep every accepted original and record changes by shot ID. No real event, attendee, notification, public venue booking or automated group is created by this fictional screenplay.

The voice sheets are ready for later voice selection and line-by-line recording/generation. No API key belongs in these Markdown files. A future Cartesia connection may supply a chosen licensed/stock voice; this package makes no actual API calls and assumes no account access.
