## Casting and attitude

One original/permitted adult Indian female voice, plausible mid-twenties, warm medium register with a small amount of natural texture. Conversational Hindi/Hinglish pronunciation, relaxed English code-switching and no actor imitation. Sleepiness means low effort and longer settling breaths, not a baby voice. Appreciation becomes brighter but still contained; dressing lines are gently amused, mirror reassurance private and kind. Do not pitch her higher to signal cuteness.

As an acting guide, ordinary phrases may sit around 115–140 words per minute with pauses; sleepy lines can use more room. These are not API speed parameters. The long central sentence has a seven-second picture window, roughly five seconds of speech and a settling smile. Audition actual recordings before locking mouth timing.

TA-L16 is gentle under the dot gesture. TA-L17 is spoken to an old friend in the evening; it is not an advertisement shouted at the viewer.
