# Tara — People Still Make an Effort

**Draft v1 · 5 November 2026 · 132 seconds / 2:12 · 36 shots · six editable batches.** Detailed script and production plan, not a finished film. No new picture or voice generation is part of this folder.

Tara is the 26-year-old corporate woman developed from the earlier Pari/sleeping-weekend idea. A dim-room wake-up, fresh air and a familiar scrolling day lead to a surprisingly thoughtful invitation. Her appreciative face becomes a visible decision: phone down, blanket aside, standing, dressing and leaving. The payoff is a shared laugh and a small new connection. She is the same person and body throughout.
