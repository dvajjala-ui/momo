# Tara — six editable batches

**132-second master plan; one new reviewed test at a time.** A batch is an editing/review group, not a request for a single long generation. The specific movement/reference test can be shorter than the whole batch. All timings are provisional edit windows.

## B01 — wake, stand, window, interest / TA01–TA06 / 00:00–00:20

**Goal:** prove she is a person moving in a real room rather than a seated face. The room brightness must have an actual window/curtain cause.

**Start:** H1 outfit, side-lying, floral blanket, lights/laptop off, curtains closed, narrow late-morning daylight seam. **End:** window open/curtain aside, electric lights still off, Tara back at the bed area with one phone, reading a shared link. The return to the bed can be an intentional cut; it must not look as if she teleported within a continuous camera move.

**Required inputs:** selected normal C face/body, dim lying keyframe, bed-edge full-body keyframe, window/handle view, accepted phone-grip reference. **Voice:** TA-L01, TA-L02, TA-L03 and beginning of TA-L04. The last line continues into B02 once; do not replay it from the start.

**Review:** feet support weight; hands press/release the mattress; head and feet stay in frame for standing; glass slides while bars remain fixed; gentle hair/curtain follow-through; brown skin remains brown in the dim and brighter light. Reject a render that only moves the camera. If the curtain and window are too much for one take, make two simple object actions and cut them.

## B02 — online yes, private contradiction, four hours / TA07–TA12 / 00:20–00:38

**Goal:** her small digital yes and bodily hesitation coexist without a moral lecture. Time passing needs new poses, not one repeated still.

**Start:** phone link read, H1, open window and naturally brighter room. **End:** warmer late-afternoon room, +4h relative to waking, same adult body/outfit, phone still available. **Voice:** end of TA-L04, TA-L05, TA-L06. **Edited inserts:** controlled interest/planning-chat text and +1h/+2h/+3h/+4h. No phone-based group joins at this stage.

**Required inputs:** phone read/neutral-screen plate, three comfortable normal poses, tumbler contact, optional separate chibi cocoon drawing. **Review:** one phone, singular cup, no object melting, no childlike body replacing Tara after the optional insert. A time label is relative elapsed time, not a second date. Her mother is not added as a wake-up device.

**Easy change:** remove the optional cocoon or a redundant pose before cutting the typed-interest/hesitation relationship. A longer water/blanket action is an extension, not filler if it makes physical continuity clearer.

## B03 — interruption and appreciative face / TA13–TA18 / 00:38–01:03

**Goal:** this contains the emotional hook, not merely an email close-up. **Start:** scrolling afternoon with phone in hand. **End:** invitation opened, phone below the frame, warm appreciative eyes/smile and the central line complete. The postcard itself is revealed in B04.

**Voice:** TA-L07/TA-L08 thought fragments, TA-L09 breath, TA-L10 central sentence. Work/friend/parent-pride thought options can be visual; do not rush a long list. **Sound:** a different gentle email sound, room quieting, stomach accent and subtle original musical warmth.

**Inputs:** afternoon light state, controlled email/time inserts, selected face close-up, actual accepted eye reaction if usable. **Review:** eyes are appreciative rather than scared; gaze stays toward the invitation; no physical paper enters the hand; identity/jewelry/hair remain stable; the sentence has real air and a smile afterward. Do not keep generating the same eye close-up instead of the needed body follow-through.

## B04 — card and action of choosing / TA19–TA24 / 01:03–01:25

**Goal:** show what prompted her response and how her choice changes her actions. **Start:** appreciative face/phone held. **End:** phone safely down, blanket aside, standing, clothes selected and one outer sleeve freed. H1 transitions toward the one approved outing outfit through clear cuts.

**Voice:** TA-L11. **Inputs:** original postcard with exact draft text/placeholders, medium-wide bed/table reference, rising pose, clothes bag, one chosen garment and sleeve-contact keyframe. **Sound:** phone/table, cloth, feet, music started by Tara, bag and sleeve movement.

**Review:** phone is placed/released before both hands work; blanket remains fabric and stays on bed; the more upright back does not thin her; outfit mismatch is held up, not a literal instantaneous body/costume morph. Real card logistics remain placeholders until confirmed. Readability can extend the insert. A sleeve/hand failure affects this action, not the accepted letter face or morning window footage.

## B05 — finishing, cap, mirror kindness / TA25–TA30 / 01:25–01:48

**Goal:** selected wardrobe stays trackable; the mirror joke is affectionate. **Start:** dressing in progress, same C hair/body. **End:** O1 complete, cap/jewelry/scarf/bag fixed, approving mirror expression and one tiny behind-ear kajal dot.

**Voice:** TA-L13, TA-L14, TA-L15, TA-L16. TA-L12 optional cap read is off by default; adding it may need more edit time. **Inputs:** approved O1 front/side/back, cap text plate, mirror geometry, original panda cutaway, safe applicator/ear close-up. **Sound:** restrained original music under clear private speech.

**Review:** no garment/strap/cap/hair teleporting; reflection matches the actual pose/details; dot appears once and stays on the same anatomical side; panda is a hard-cut imagined drawing, not a human face morph or weight joke. The alternative maroon outfit does not leak into the white/blue choice. Change the cap text in the graphic insert rather than redesigning her whole face.

## B06 — ready, out, a memory / TA31–TA36 / 01:48–02:12

**Goal:** payoff remains a human shared moment, with clear story time and voluntary further connection. **Start:** same outfit/dot, approving nod. **End:** paper/crayon brand card after a small fictional café glimpse and evening voice note. **Voice:** TA-L17 only; optional UI questions are visual text, not a narrator.

**Inputs:** doorway/shoes/key/phone/bag state; fictional café/table adults with their own accepted references; evening room; optional attendee-only group UI; original end card. Keep the café cast small rather than asking for an unprepared six-character crossover.

**Review:** body stays the same person; phone/keys/bag all have a consistent owner; the evening jump is explicit; no actual customer/event claim; the voice note sounds personal. If the attendee-group terms cannot be read, extend the insert or omit that concept stage. Do not imply an interest tap proved attendance or silently exposed phone numbers.

## Length changes

| Change | New planning length | What to preserve |
|---|---:|---|
| Default | 132 s | Full core sequence; cap phrase visual, short thought fragments |
| Add optional spoken cap read | about 134–135 s | Reply remains unhurried; only that shot/downstream timecodes move |
| Let postcard/group screen read longer | about 136–142 s | Complete practical information and genuine choice |
| Fuller work/friends thought fragments | add about 3–5 s | No fast internal monologue; show one thought at a time |
| Tighten later short cut | separate 45–60 s editorial version | Desire → invitation → physical choice → shared memory; do not overwrite this master |

Shorten repeated scrolling setup, optional mismatch/cocoon or forgotten-phone gag first. Do not solve a duration problem by shrinking pauses until the central line and mirror kindness sound mechanical. Keep all actual source takes so extensions and alternative jokes are reversible.

## Revision dependencies

Face/body/hair changes affect every dependent shot and must be treated as a new identity version. O1 changes affect TA24/TA25 onward, including mirror/café/return. Invitation graphics affect TA14/TA19 and any real-world logistics text. A voice re-record affects its visible mouth/window, not unrelated room actions. Track after-edit timecodes separately from source voice IDs and raw take IDs.
