# AD02 — Steam Se Baat Tak — momo edition

**Draft v1 · 00:30 planned edit · 6 shots · fictional script only.**

## Purpose and story

A food-edition ad where the attractive momo leads to a human conversation. The invitation is to explore a small plan, not order delivery or assume a specific restaurant partnership.

Steam catches attention → share with permission → a tiny waiting joke → two people start talking → momo edition end card.
