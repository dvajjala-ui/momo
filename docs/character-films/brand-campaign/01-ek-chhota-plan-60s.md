# AD01 — Ek Chhota Plan — brand film

**Draft v1 · 01:00 planned edit · 12 shots · fictional script only.**

## Purpose and story

Introduce Momo to Pizza through a small act of showing up and sharing a table. One simple CTA, no testimonial or claim that the brand guarantees a friend group.

0–15: three small decisions. 15–30: meeting becomes physical. 30–45: specific shared moments. 45–55: a warmer ordinary table. 55–60: brand and invitation to explore.

## Picture, cast and wardrobe

Fictional Ahmedabad homes, public café and short riverfront walk on separate days; cuts deliberately mark separate situations.

All proposed casual wardrobe IDs from the ensemble bible. Tara opening uses existing lavender home kit and a later deliberate cut to TA-CAS1; no magical outfit change.

Read the full [ensemble cast and wardrobe guide](../ensemble-series/02-CAST-WARDROBE-AND-VOICE.md), [original character library](../README.md) and [location register](../ensemble-series/03-AHMEDABAD-LOCATIONS.md). All people are adults with distinct references and stable proportions. No new model or audio is created here.

## Sound and performance

Use each established character voice with exact line IDs below. One speaker at a time, listeners genuinely reacting. Music is original/cleared, low during speech and absent where a pause carries the joke. Sound follows real material contact. Exact mouth timing waits for measured accepted audio. For vertical use, reframe the two-person composition deliberately rather than cropping the listener out.
