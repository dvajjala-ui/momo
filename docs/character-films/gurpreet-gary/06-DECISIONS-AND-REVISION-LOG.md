## New story proposals

One Sunday early-morning game finishes. Old teammates leave for ordinary reasons, and Gary waves back. During a normal water/rest break he notices hunger and an opportunity for a small food-and-chat plan. He joins or expresses interest, reviews logistics, changes footwear in a clear bench cut, then meets a few original fictional participants at a public food spot. The connection grows through a listening beat, a napkin tactics joke and another person's better joke. An invitation to a later casual Sunday is freely offered and practically confirmed. A second-Sunday time cue, a short pass and water break show a modest continuation.

**Venue and time:** urban Ahmedabad-inspired turf/public food setting, with specific names, access, transport, host, date, full cost, capacity and end time unfilled. Do not assume that every location is open early or that the city has approved the activity. This script is a fictional production concept, not an actual operational invitation.

**Rendering language:** original cinematic 3D as in the current short-series source. If the whole series later adopts another style, preserve the underlying identity and review a deliberate adaptation; do not let a shot silently turn Gary into another age/body or copy Tara's exact face.
