# Bhaap — supporting mascot bible

**Supporting original mascot, not a seventh human lead or a separate promised two-minute film.** The latest source uses the working name Bhaap, meaning steam. In the new long films the humans carry desire, choice and dialogue; the mascot is an optional small visual signature, not a magical machine that guarantees friends.

## Shape and visual identity

A small steamed-dumpling-inspired cream silhouette, gently rounded base, a pinched top with a few readable pleat ridges and one soft curl of steam above it. Face is minimal: two small dark eyes, small relaxed mouth and restrained brow/cheek accents. Keep it original; no copied branded dumpling, studio face or franchise accessory. Its material is soft food/cartoon paper, not wet photorealistic skin.

Working palette: warm cream `#F1E6CF`, soft beige pleat shadow `#D9C4A4`, warm dark line/eyes `#46352B`. Exact swatches are proposals. The steam curl has one coherent upward arc and dissipates gently; it is not a permanent flaming crown or a smoking head.

Front and three-quarter views preserve the same pleat count/silhouette. Side/back do not grow new limbs or a second face. If simple little arms are chosen for a paper-doodle version, lock them on that version's sheet rather than adding/removing them between adjacent shots. The normal mascot does not become a different food in the pizza edition.

## Scale and placement

Use it in an original paper-margin, invitation doodle, controlled brand insert or an explicitly imagined tiny comic accent. The size is consistent with that graphic. It should not suddenly become a human-sized talking creature inside an otherwise grounded room. One cameo is enough; repeated magical screen popping should not replace the character's body action or product logic.

The second food edition can use a small original pizza accent or chai steam nearby. It is not a competing named mascot and does not require a menu catalogue in each film.

## Behavior and emotion keys

- Quiet interest: tiny head/pleat tilt, steam curl drifts.
- Acknowledgment: one small nod, then stillness.
- Amusement: eyes narrow gently and mouth lifts, no giant teeth.
- Warm welcome: a little open pose in the chosen doodle version, not a pointing sales gesture.
- End-card settle: curl resolves and body settles softly, leaving text space.

No guilt, false urgency, safety badge or thumbs-up implying verified identity/attendance. A mascot wink cannot stand in for a real host confirming a public plan.

## Voice and sound

Default new long-film cameo is silent, with a tiny original paper/steam accent only if it fits. If a future speaking version is wanted, it needs one consistent original/permitted voice, a separate voice alias and reviewed short lines. Do not make it sound like a specific cartoon child or use it to announce guaranteed joining counts. No voice is generated here.

## Reusable reference prompt

```text
Original small steamed-dumpling mascot Bhaap, warm cream rounded dumpling body with a few simple stable top pleats, tiny dark expressive eyes and a small relaxed mouth, one gentle rising steam curl. Friendly restrained paper/cartoon visual signature, no logo or franchise resemblance, no flame, no photorealistic human skin, no giant teeth. Neutral front, three-quarter, side and back views with the same silhouette and face construction. Leave clean space around it and do not bake readable words into the artwork. Keep it a supporting brand doodle rather than a new human-scale creature.
```
