# Future custom member episode — reusable planning form

This template is for a future consenting participant, not an invented seventh person or a finished film. The six default films are in their named folders. Do not add a real person's likeness, voice or personal story without their willing participation and review.

## Before writing

- Working character name, adult age, chosen pronouns and how they want to appear.
- Their own small interest/plan and what actually happened, if based on a real meetup.
- Original fictional design or specifically permitted visual reference; no photo is required to participate.
- What may be public, what stays private, whether they want an original stock voice, and how they can change their mind before publication.
- Final story/design review by that participant before any public post. Consent to an episode is not consent to voice cloning or unlimited reuse.

## Folder pattern

Create one new named folder containing overview, detailed character bible, complete screenplay, individually timed shots, voice/sound ledger, editable batches and decisions/revision notes. Copy the structure, not another person's face, cultural details or entire story.

## Character detail form

Record face construction from front/three-quarter/profile, skin undertone under different light, eyes/brows/nose/mouth, fixed hair silhouette and length, adult body build/scale, clothing seams/layers/fasteners/footwear/accessories, handedness/prop continuity, gestures, expression keys and behavior. Distinguish the participant's choices, observed reference details and the designer's proposals. A personality label is not a performance description.

## Two-plus-minute story skeleton

| Draft edit | Dramatic work | Required physical action |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:20 | Specific ordinary desire and individual world | One observable action, not an explanatory biography |
| 00:20–00:45 | Small practical obstacle or timing mismatch | Something handled/attempted, with room for a joke |
| 00:45–01:10 | A voluntary plan/interest invitation | A choice made after readable practical information |
| 01:10–01:40 | Preparation and movement toward the plan | Packing, dressing, travel/threshold or another specific action |
| 01:40–02:10 | Shared experience, one ordinary human payoff | A mutual action/listener reaction rather than brand applause |
| 02:10–02:20 | Memory or optional next step | Clear choice, original end graphic or private reflection |

This is a 140-second planning starting point, not a requirement to narrate every second or render a whole film at once. Tailor the structure to the person's actual story and remove unsupported product claims.

## Line and shot IDs

Choose a unique prefix before writing; do not reuse TA/DH/RO/GA/SA/ME IDs for a different person. Each shot has a continuous edit time, batch, purpose, frame/camera, start state, one action, exact dialogue ID or silence, sound, end state, text/edit insert and a detailed prompt. Each line has exact text, language form, pronunciation, emotion, pause and estimated window before audio exists.

## Review batches

Plan about five groups of 25–30 edited seconds. One accepted reference and voice test first; review the first actual action before making the rest. Record downstream impact when the outfit, voice or story changes. Keep original takes and use a separate short cut if needed rather than overwriting the long story.

No photos, voice key, API integration, actual generation, invitation send or public post is performed by this template.
