# Sameer — character and visual bible

**Film:** Make a wish · **Age:** 25 · **Status:** long-form writing proposal built around an existing original character sheet. Physical refinements below interpret that sheet; the actual reference wins if prose appears to conflict with it.

## Reference authority and silhouette

Use [the original corrected character sheet](../../video-assets/sameer-character-sheet-v2.png). It shows front, three-quarter, profile and back views, plus six expressions. Preserve the warm brown face, full black curls, light stubble, mustard hoodie, olive cargo trousers, plain white low-top sneakers and charcoal backpack with original woven patches. Do not use the superseded sheet containing a swoosh-like shoe mark. Do not replace his face with a new generic handsome lead. The existing thirty-second pass is an imperfect source, not a finished master or evidence that every new action has rendered.

The full-body silhouette is an ordinary adult of average build: head carried above a relaxed neck, shoulders narrower than Gary's, ribs and pelvis aligned without an exaggerated V shape. A slightly loose hoodie rounds the shoulders and gathers softly at the waist. Cargo legs taper at the ankle; shoes have a modest flat sole. The backpack is compact enough that its lower edge sits above the seat of the trousers. His visual rhythm is curls → warm hoodie → quieter olive legs → simple white feet. Never make him much taller, thinner or more muscular between shots. Do not enlarge the eyes/head until he reads as a child.

## Face construction from different views

- **Front:** a soft oval face with gently wider upper cheeks and a rounded, rather than pointed, chin. The jaw is visible but not squared like Gary's. The cheek is fuller when he smiles; it is not a separate weight change. The mouth sits naturally above a little stubble on the chin, with room for a small lower-lip shadow.
- **Three-quarter:** the far cheek and eye narrow with perspective. Preserve the same rounded cheek-to-jaw connection; do not turn the near cheek into a long sharp plane. The nose projects gently and the mouth wraps around the muzzle plane instead of sliding onto a flat sticker face.
- **Profile:** a modestly projecting nose with a rounded tip, a natural brow-to-bridge transition and a softly rounded chin. Keep nose/chin relationship identical to the sheet. The upper lip is not aggressively protruding. A little stubble edge describes the jaw without becoming a dense beard.
- **Back:** the curl mass covers the upper nape; no long ponytail, undercut or sharply shaved hairline is introduced. Neck and hoodie collar sit together naturally. The backpack straps pass over the hoodie, not through the head or hood.
- **Eyes:** dark brown irises, dark pupils, clear small catchlights. The approved style uses large expressive eyes, but eyelids still narrow in a smile and lower during a relaxed blink. Curiosity is a brow lift and forward eye focus; it is not permanently enormous pupils or a shocked open mouth.
- **Brows:** thick dark brows, a mostly soft arch and visible inner brow movement. Brief disappointment can lift the inner ends slightly, then release. Do not keep a deep central frown over every shot; that was a weakness of the earlier pass.
- **Nose:** preserve the reference's medium bridge and softly rounded tip. Nostril shapes respond to angle but do not expand into a comic caricature. There is no nose stud or invented scar.
- **Mouth:** medium upper lip and slightly fuller lower lip, corners capable of an uneven private smile. Teeth appear only in a real laugh or a clear phoneme. No permanently exposed bright tooth strip. Lips return to rest during silent listening.
- **Stubble:** short and light, concentrated around upper lip, chin and lower jaw, with skin still visible. It must not turn into a clean-shaven face in one shot or a full beard in the next. Ear and cheek stubble boundaries follow the reference.

### Skin, material and working color notes

Keep his brown complexion in shade as well as sun. Warm light may change the light falling on skin, not turn him porcelain. Match the actual reference before locking a final palette. Skin is softly shaded 3D animated material with restrained subsurface warmth, no glossy wax, photoreal pores or painted-on blush hearts.

## Hair, neck and hands

His dense black curls make a rounded crown with uneven clustered edges. Preserve the main forehead curl silhouette, temple fullness and short nape. Curls move as a few connected clumps, settling after a turn; they are not dozens of independent springs. Wind at the riverside lifts only the loosest tips. The direction of his part/curl mass remains consistent after mirror-like angle changes. Do not attach a cap, headband or sunglasses simply to hide difficult hair.

Neck movements begin from the sternum and shoulders: a small forward lean when interested, a supported recline against the pillow, an upright easy stance outside. Hands are adult, with readable knuckles and five fingers. Phone grip is usually left hand supporting the lower half, right thumb interacting; an edited change is allowed if recorded. He does not suddenly type with six fingers. When holding a flask or bag strap he releases it before performing a second conflicting action.

## Wardrobe K1 — Saturday home / planning

Sameer already wears the same unbranded mustard hoodie and olive cargos seen on the sheet. This keeps the film close to the locked design. At home he may have the backpack on a chair, not constantly on his back. Shoes are beside the bed at first, not worn under the blanket.

- Mid-weight cotton hoodie, matte rather than shiny. Two drawstrings hang from the same eyelets. A single kangaroo pocket has two hand openings; no zip is introduced across the chest. Ribbed cuffs sit at wrists, and a ribbed lower band gathers softly at the hips.
- The hood lies folded at the upper back. It does not repeatedly flip up between shots. When leaning on the bed, the hood compresses against the pillow rather than clipping through it.
- Olive cargo trousers have one consistent side-pocket arrangement, clear waistband and simple tapered cuffs. Use the sheet's seam positions as the layout; do not add pouches at random. No camouflage print or military insignia.
- Plain white/off-white low-top sneakers, simple laces and toe panel, no swoosh/stripe/wordmark. Shoe shape, lace color and sole thickness stay consistent. Socks are low neutral ankle socks. Both feet have matching shoes.
- No watch, chain, rings, earpods or eyeglasses are required. An accessory may be proposed later, but adding one halfway through a batch is a continuity error.

## Wardrobe K2 — Sunday sunrise walk

K2 is **K1 plus the existing backpack, shoes and socks**, not a different fashion makeover. In the deliberate preparing-to-leave cut, he puts on shoes, checks the bag and adjusts both shoulder straps. The charcoal backpack has a top loop, two shoulder straps, dark zips and the original two small woven patch motifs. They must remain abstract original patterns, not text, political symbols or brand logos. The patches cannot migrate to another side of the bag between front/back views. The central diamond-shaped patch and lower rectangular woven panel visible on the reference establish placement.

The walk is a comfortable urban riverside stroll, so do not add hiking poles, crampons, mountain boots, expedition straps or a rope. Carry a small water bottle and a folded light cloth in the bag. The steel chai flask belongs to a supporting participant at the meetup unless a later shot explicitly transfers it. No participant drinks hot chai while actively walking on stairs.

## Behavioral design: what he does

Sameer notices possibilities: he watches someone shape clay, follows the light in a sunrise image and sketches the shape of a plan on a small piece of paper. He enjoys having an idea before he has a group. When friends cannot join, he checks the message, pauses and puts the phone on his chest for a moment. He is not angry at them or diagnosed by the story. He sits up because he thinks of a practical next step, not because an app casts a spell.

He makes room for others physically: shifts a cup so another person can set something down, steps half a pace aside on the walkway, waits for a person finishing a sentence. These small actions show welcome without writing a fixed personality label. At first he rehearses a greeting in his breath; later the same greeting comes out easily. He is allowed to enjoy quiet stretches of the walk without the film claiming silence is a problem.

His comedy comes from overpreparing a small outing: packing two unnecessary napkins, making a tiny route doodle that needs no heroic equipment, then realizing a simple water bottle is enough. He can smile at himself. Do not humiliate him with a cruel rejection montage or make old friends look heartless for being busy.

## Eight expression keys

| Key | Eyes and brows | Mouth / breath | Body |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curious | Eyes track one detail; outer brow lifts | Closed soft mouth, small inhale | Torso moves a few centimeters forward |
| Enjoying an idea | Lower lids rise with smile | One corner lifts, then both | Fingers stop scrolling |
| Brief disappointment | Inner brow lifts once | Exhale; lips soften | Shoulders settle, no dramatic collapse |
| Considering | Gaze moves from phone to ceiling | Lips pressed lightly, then release | One hand rests on chest/blanket |
| Practical decision | Eyes refocus, brow smooths | Tiny “theek” shape | Feet under body; sits forward |
| Pleasant surprise | Eyelids open moderately | Small breath, not a scream | Chin lifts slightly |
| First greeting | Gaze meets the other person, then relaxes | Small open smile | Bag strap held with one hand |
| Shared laugh | Eyes narrow naturally | Teeth visible briefly; breathing breaks | Shoulders move, one hand protects the cup |

## Five signature poses

1. Propped against a pillow, one knee relaxed, phone low enough to see his face; the hoodie bunches under his ribs rather than changing torso length.
2. Seated forward on bed edge, elbows briefly on thighs, idea arriving through the lifted head.
3. Standing at the door with two backpack straps settled and feet shoulder-width apart, checking one pocket.
4. Meeting someone with a small open palm at waist height, never grabbing an unfamiliar person's shoulder.
5. Sitting at a breakfast table, body turned toward the speaker, cup parked safely before laughter.
