## Long-form choices proposed here

- **Title / runtime:** Make a wish, 150 seconds, thirty shots, five editable thirty-second batches.
- **Wish:** an easy public sunrise riverside walk followed by optional breakfast. Exact public meeting point/access hours, host, date, route accessibility, end time and cost remain placeholders for a real plan.
- **Chronology:** Saturday evening inspiration → later same-evening interest/logistics → next-morning meeting → a short level-paved walk → seated chai/breakfast. The edit must display the overnight jump.
- **Register:** curious and gently humorous. Brief disappointment does not become a permanently worried frown. Packing a basic water bottle as though preparing an expedition supplies one small self-aware joke.
- **Participation:** a few original fictional adults attend after choosing to join. No real testimonial, live sign-up count or promise that posting a wish always fills it.
- **Photo:** he asks, someone freely agrees, then a photo may be taken. Anyone can remain out of frame. The film does not imply permission to post a real person's image publicly.
- **Next hobby:** “Agli baar pottery?” is a conversation, not a confirmed event or an automatically generated itinerary.
- **Narration:** character-led by default, exact voice cues in the voice sheet; no required series narrator or guaranteed-gang claim.
