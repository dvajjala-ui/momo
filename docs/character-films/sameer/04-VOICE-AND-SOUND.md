Conversational urban Hindi/Hinglish; Ahmedabad is an environment, not a direction to exaggerate an accent. His English words belong naturally in his sentence. Avoid a rich movie-trailer bass or a childish bright squeak. Start around 118–132 spoken words per minute for isolated lines, with genuine pauses; this is a performance reference, not a provider parameter. He can speak less than a sentence over five seconds. Keep one lead voice across home, street and café, varying distance/room response rather than choosing another speaker.
