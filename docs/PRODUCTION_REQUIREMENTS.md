## Product decisions already made

- Use the existing React, Vinext, Cloudflare Workers and D1 architecture.
- Keep both momo and pizza editions and the crayon/scrapbook character.
- Use device sessions for low-friction access. Do not rely on IP addresses as permanent identity.
- The first 100-member pilot and every displayed count must be truthful. A queue can be enabled and managed by the host.
- Reference images in `assest/` are visual inspiration unless the owner confirms publication rights. Create original paper, doodle, emblem and envelope treatments for the product UI.
- Sidekicks can submit an application, but they receive no automatic role or special product privileges.
- The host can send private in-site notes. External email or WhatsApp sending stays separately gated.

## Name options

1. **Momo to Pizza**: direct, memorable and covers both editions.
2. **Momo, Maybe Pizza**: playful and conversational.
3. **Momo & a Slice**: shorter and warm.
4. **From Momo to Pizza**: suggests plans growing from one small idea.

The implementation uses **Momo to Pizza** until the owner chooses another option.
