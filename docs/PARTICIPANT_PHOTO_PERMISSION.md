## Permission wording

> I have seen the identified photo and choose whether Momo may publish it on the channels listed above for the stated use. I can say no without affecting my participation. I can later ask Momo to remove copies it controls by using the listed withdrawal contact. Momo will explain that copies already downloaded or reshared by others may not be fully recoverable.
