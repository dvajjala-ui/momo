# The whole menu: different cravings, same table

Production brief · 5 October 2026

This is a film brief, not a generated movie. The old slideshow has been retired.

## Tool and reference

Use [Google Flow](https://flow.google.com/) or [Runway](https://app.runwayml.com/). Runway’s current Gen-4.5 guide supports 2–10-second clips. Check the options and cost in your own account. No paid generation has been started.

Use `whole-menu.png` as a character reference. Build a scene-specific first frame rather than animating the horizontal lineup. The desired result has independently moving characters, physical props and a changing camera perspective.

## Master prompt

Create a 5-second cinematic animated ensemble short starring original anthropomorphic foods: a cheese pizza slice, Mumbai vada pav with a green chilli companion, a noodle bowl, a spiral-pasta bowl, chai and a tiny coffee cup, and a dal-baati plate. Use the supplied food character sheet for identity and colour, translated to tactile stop-motion clay with hand-painted pencil edges. No dumplings or momos. Real character movement and changing camera perspective, not a slideshow or a still-image zoom.

0.0–1.1 seconds: close on a chai cup setting down a work bag. A phone displays only a simple envelope icon; the cup smiles and looks toward warm café light.
1.1–3.3 seconds: a quick continuous tracking shot along a miniature Ahmedabad-inspired lane. Pizza and vada pav exchange a high-five; noodle and pasta bowls lift a café curtain; dal baati makes room at the table. Distinct silhouettes, coherent screen direction, no morphing between foods.
3.3–5.0 seconds: the camera settles around the shared table as chai and coffee arrive. Someone deals the first card. Friends pass a plate and laugh at the same small moment. Leave an empty seat facing the viewer.

Warm evening practical lighting, miniature-paper street details, handcrafted textures, natural squash and stretch, shallow depth of field. Joyful grown-up nostalgia. Sound: street ambience, café bell, cups clink, chair scrape, card shuffle. Compose a 9:16 master and a separate 16:9 version. Keep the final 0.7 seconds calm for an editor to add “different cravings. same table.” and “a Momo weekend.” Render without generated text.

## Shot-by-shot workflow

| Shot | Final edit | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A small decision | 0–1.2 sec | A working-day action ends; a face responds to the thought of a plan. |
| The way there | 1.2–3.4 sec | Low tracking shot into a warm café; distinct characters move with believable weight. |
| The shared table | 3.4–5 sec | A plate passes, a card lands, a small laugh, an empty chair for the viewer. |

Generate each shot separately with extra duration, then select the strongest five seconds. For image-to-video, focus the prompt on motion and camera. If too much happens to read clearly, simplify the middle shot instead of speeding everything up.

## Delivery and review

- Separate 9:16 Instagram and 16:9 website compositions. Keep faces and text crop-safe.
- Original or licensed sound only: café bell, footsteps, chair scrape, card shuffle. No recognisable film songs without rights.
- Add “kal milte hain?” and the wordmark in the editor; do not rely on generated text.
- Export a full-quality master plus H.264 web copy, with a muted website version.
- Reject still-image pans, food morphing, cloned faces, extra limbs, flickering props or fake celebrity cameos.
- Do not label anyone lazy, shy or awkward. The film invites people; it does not diagnose them.
- Only add an actual date, venue and cost once confirmed. Label concept posts as a preview.
